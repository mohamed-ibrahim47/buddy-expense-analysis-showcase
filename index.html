Home

The whole month at a glance

Open the app and know where you stand before you've finished your coffee.

  • A running line of the month's spending against an even pace, and where the month is heading.
  • Where it went: your biggest categories, with a red "3× usual" badge on any running far above normal.
  • Money left last month? One tap puts it in a Savings wallet, so it stays saved instead of quietly disappearing.
  • Step back through earlier months, hide the total like a banking app, and see the next payments due.
  • A setup tour that starts with your categories and income, and a tip behind every ⓘ.
Money in & out

Cards, instalments and gam'eya, sorted

What's left this month after everything that's still due, and every commitment in one place.

  • Credit cards as a wallet stack. Tap a card to bring it forward: amount owed, next statement and limit.
  • Instalments and loans with a ring of months paid and the next payment.
  • Gam'eya with a dot for every month and a star on your payout turn.
  • Pay full or minimum, monthly repeats added by themselves, money you lent tracked until it's back.
Analysis

Learns what's normal for you

Not generic advice: everything is measured against your own history.

  • Your average month, and each category this month next to its usual.
  • Where the money went for all time or any year, share of each month, and a trend per category.
  • Unusual months flagged, fixed costs against variable ones, and what you saved each month, including how much of a good month paid back an earlier overspend.
  • Sub-categories broken down too: pharmacy, doctor, visits.
Adding & history

Circle a few amounts, see what they add up to

Adding an expense is a category, an amount and Save. Looking back is just as quick.

  • Circle to sum. Hold, then draw a loop around amounts in History, like circling something to search it. The total shows straight away.
  • Sub-categories that keep up: your most used come first, the rest A to Z, and a new one is one tap away.
  • A change log for every amount: when it was added, raised, moved or typed in Excel, as clear before-and-after cards.
  • Big numbers welcome: amounts into the billions, with commas as you type.
Bank SMS & wallets

From a bank message to an expense in two taps

Share or paste your bank's messages, English or Arabic. The app finds the amount, the shop and the date. You choose the category.

  • Purchases, withdrawals and transfers, grouped by day. Ignore several at once with a long-press.
  • Money in goes in as income or into one of your wallets.
  • Wallets for pocket money or a trip. A picked wallet never goes below zero.
  • Nothing is added by itself. Messages are read on the phone and never leave it.
Excel is the database

Every amount is a cell you can see

One sheet per month, one column per category. Sub-categories are cell notes, and hidden sheets keep the app's log and settings. Typed something straight into Excel? The app picks it up. Every save is checked before it replaces your file, and a backup is kept.

How it's built

Small, careful, offline

Kotlin + Compose

Material 3 with the Night Sky look: four swipeable tabs and a side menu.

Apache POI

Reads and writes the workbook. Every save is checked before it replaces the file.

Pure-Kotlin core

All money logic (wallets, carry-over, cards, gam'eya, SMS parsing) in one tested module.

Two builds

One that reads the SMS inbox, and one without any SMS permission that installs anywhere.
